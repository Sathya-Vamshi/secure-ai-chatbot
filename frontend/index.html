About

What Aegis is

Aegis is a chat assistant you can hand a document to and ask questions about it. Everything you upload and every conversation you have is private to your own account — no one else can see your documents or your chats unless you explicitly share access with them.

How each part works

💬

Chat

Ask questions in plain English. If you've uploaded a document, Aegis reads just the relevant part of it (not the whole thing) to answer quickly and accurately. You can also just chat normally — no document required. Messages that look like an attempt to trick the AI into ignoring its rules are automatically blocked and logged.

⚡

Fast, right-sized answers

Aegis routes each question to the model built for it — a quick model for simple lookups, a deeper one for genuinely complex questions — and keeps that model warm between messages instead of reloading it every time. Small talk, document word/letter counts, and questions about Aegis itself are answered instantly with no model call at all, since the answer is always the same.

🪪

Aegis knows itself

Ask "what is this?" or "what can you do?" and Aegis answers from a fixed set of facts about itself — never from whatever a loaded document happens to say. A document can describe anything it wants; it can never talk Aegis into believing it's a different tool.

🗂️

Multiple chats

Click "New chat" any time to start a fresh conversation without losing the old one. All your past chats are listed in the sidebar — click any of them to jump back in, or hover and click ✕ to delete one for good.

📄

Documents & tamper detection

When you upload a file, Aegis fingerprints it (a SHA-256 hash) and keeps a backup copy. Every time it's used, that fingerprint is re-checked against the file on disk. If the two don't match, the file was changed outside the app — Aegis blocks it immediately instead of ever answering from tampered content, and you can restore the trusted backup with one click.

🕓

Versions

Re-uploading a file with the same name doesn't overwrite it — it saves a new version and keeps the old one, so nothing is ever silently lost.

🔑

Authorization keys (Team & Access)

Only you can edit a document you own. To let a teammate make a change to it, generate a one-time authorization key for them — it can be limited to a number of uses or an expiry date. They use that key to submit their edit, which is saved as a new version, never overwriting the original.

🛡️

Audit Log (owner account)

Every upload, edit, authorization key, login, and blocked/suspicious action is recorded with a timestamp — a full history to check if something ever needs investigating.

🔒

Privacy between accounts

Your documents, your document list, and your chat history are visible only to you. The one exception is a document a teammate has deliberately shared with you using an authorization key — that document (and only that one) becomes visible to you too.

🎨

Dark & light themes

Flip the switch in the sidebar (or top-right on the welcome screen) to swap between the dark theme and the bright theme — your choice is remembered on this device.

Privacy & Permissions

🕓

System date & time access

Off by default. A language model has no real clock, so Aegis never lets it guess the date or time — it only ever answers from this system's actual clock, and only once you've explicitly allowed it. You'll also be asked inline the first time you ask a date/time question, if you'd rather decide then.
